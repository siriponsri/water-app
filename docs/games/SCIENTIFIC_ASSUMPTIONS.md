# Scientific Assumptions and Training Boundary

1. All lot numbers, samples, observations, and outcomes are fictional educational states.
2. The bundled profile is explicitly `educationalOnly`; exact strains, challenge levels, incubation windows, quantitative acceptance rules, and selective/indicative expectations require verification against the current licensed compendium and approved site SOP.
3. Growth-promotion testing, method suitability, and routine product testing are separate questions.
4. Negative-control growth, traceability mismatch, or an out-of-profile read prevents an uncomplicated release decision in the deterministic engine.
5. TSB and RV are broths. SDA is used as a fungal recovery/observation clue. MSA, MAC, and XLD are selective/differential plates.
6. Culture reactions narrow a hypothesis but do not prove species identity. Case 01 supports a presumptive salt-tolerant, mannitol-positive Staphylococcus-like conclusion only; confirmation is required.
7. The scene is a controlled visualization. Colony emergence and turbidity are not real kinetic or predictive biological models.
8. Scientific evaluation is deterministic and offline. No LLM judges learner correctness.
